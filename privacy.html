Public policy

Privacy Policy.

Effective July 15, 2026

Gonvex Auth is the shared authentication service used by applications powered by Gonvex. This policy explains the information handled when you choose to sign in with Google through Gonvex Auth.

Information we receive

When you approve Google sign-in, Gonvex Auth receives the basic identity information authorized by you: your Google account identifier, email address, email-verification status, display name, and profile image. We also process security and operational information such as sign-in time, requesting application, request metadata, and authentication events.

How information is used

  • To verify your identity and create a session for the application that requested sign-in.
  • To associate you with the correct Gonvex project, tenant, invitations, and memberships.
  • To detect abuse, investigate authentication failures, and protect accounts.
  • To operate, debug, and improve the reliability of Gonvex Auth.

Project-scoped access

A Gonvex Auth session is scoped to the requesting application. Signing in to one application does not grant access to the Gonvex administration dashboard or to unrelated Gonvex projects.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Identity information is shared only with the Gonvex-powered application you chose to access, service providers required to operate the authentication infrastructure, or when required for security or legal compliance. Google processes information under its own privacy terms.

Retention and security

Identity, membership, session, and audit information is retained only as needed to provide authentication, maintain security, and meet operational or legal obligations. Secrets and session credentials are protected using access controls and secure transport. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may decline Google sign-in, revoke the connection from your Google Account, or contact the operator of the application you used to request access, correction, or deletion of associated account information.

Questions

For privacy or authentication questions, contact the operator of the application that directed you to Gonvex Auth. Technical and open-source inquiries may be submitted through the Gonvex issue tracker.
